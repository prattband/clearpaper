ClearPaperA research prototype from the Making the State Understandable project

Find out what a government notice wants from you

Add a photo, scan or PDF of a notice. ClearPaper lists the steps in order, the documents to prepare, and what the notice leaves unclear. Every step shows the line it came from, so you can check it against the original.

Add photos or a PDF of the notice Up to 5 files. A PDF can be up to 4 MB. Several photos can be pages of one notice.
